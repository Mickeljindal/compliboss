The problem

Compliance is blocking your next deal — and eating your team alive.

If you sell to enterprise, a SOC 2 or ISO 27001 report is table stakes. But getting there the old way means months of manual work that pulls engineers off the roadmap.

Deals stall in security review

Prospects ask for your SOC 2 report before they’ll sign. No report, no deal — or a 3-month delay while you scramble.

Evidence lives in spreadsheets

Screenshots, exports, and “who has access to what” tracked by hand — stale the moment they’re captured.

Audits become fire drills

The week before the audit, everyone drops everything to chase documents that should have been collected all along.

Drift happens silently

A misconfigured bucket, an expired cert, an ex-employee who still has access — and nobody notices until it matters.
